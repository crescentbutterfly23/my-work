Read me first

Every entry links to a real product page. Licensing differs by source — Mr.Mockup and MockupTree state free personal and commercial use; Magnific’s free license allows commercial use only with visible attribution (see the footer). Style, business, setting and customization tags are estimates derived from each mockup's title — good for narrowing a long list, not a guarantee. Confirm the license and what's actually editable before client work.

Downloads happen on the source site — Mr.Mockup has a download button on each product page, MockupTree routes through a free cart. Neither allows a direct-file link, so “Go to download” lands on the page holding the PSD.
